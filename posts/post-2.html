"What is your purpose in life?"

Is it to become the greatest fighter of all time, to amass wealth, or to be the best athlete of your generation?

[Story]
For a young boy named John, his purpose was to achieve the seemingly impossible task of winning three FIFA world cup. This aspiration, while daunting, gave him direction and meaning.

THE NATURE OF DREAMS

For many of us, our dreams often remain just that—dreams. They can feel like distant goals that are just out of reach. However, every individual has a purpose, and what sets us apart is how we choose to pursue it. John understood that he had no other choice; he wanted to be the best football player in the world. He found his purpose in a challenge that seemed insurmountable, which ultimately gave his life meaning.

THE STORY OF THE KING AND THE BOULDER

To illustrate the concept of purpose, consider the story of a king tasked by Zeus to roll a boulder to the top of a mountain as punishment for his reckless behavior. The king's freedom depended on completing this seemingly impossible task. Despite failing multiple times, he persevered. The question arises: what other option did he have? Giving up was not an option.

THE ILLUSION OF REACHING THE TOP

Interestingly, the king never actually reaches the top of the mountain. This reflects a profound truth about life: while we may strive for our goals, there will always be a higher mountain to climb. This realization is not meant to be nihilistic; rather, it emphasizes the importance of the journey over the destination.

FINDING MEANING IN THE JOURNEY

The ancient Greeks who told this story overlooked a crucial detail: the king smiles despite his curse. He understands that he has two choices: to accept his fate and give up or to find purpose in the task itself rather than the end result. This perspective is vital for living a fulfilling life.

ACCEPTING LIFE'S LACK OF MEANING

At the end of the day, we must accept that life may not have inherent meaning. Our goals, dreams, and aspirations serve as coping mechanisms to navigate this reality. They provide us with purpose in a world that can often feel purposeless. The king smiles because he focuses on improving each day, striving to get higher up the mountain than he did yesterday.

CONCLUSION

In conclusion, the meaning of life may lie in accepting that there is no ultimate meaning and instead finding significance within ourselves. It is about the daily efforts we make and the pride we take in our personal growth. Ultimately, it is not the achievement of our goals that defines us, but the journey we undertake to pursue them.

"The meaning of your life is to accept that there is no real meaning. Instead, find the meaning within yourself instead of material world, and the reason behind your smile."

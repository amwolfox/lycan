The Bhagavad Gita is a guide to the ultimate survival of the soul (Atman) and navigating the mental and emotional battles of life. It teaches that the true self is eternal, while equipping you to face daily struggles with peace and purpose.

Whenever you feel low or experience a difficult emotion, don't try to escape it. Instead, study it. Ask yourself why it's arising, what triggered it, and what effects it has on your thoughts and actions. With patient observation and understanding, you'll gradually rise above it instead of being controlled by it.

"The unreal has no existence, and the real never ceases to be."
